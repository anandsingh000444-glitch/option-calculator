Δ (change) actual Black-Scholes repricing se calculate hota hai — sirf Delta approximation nahi, isliye Gamma/Vega ka effect bhi included hai. Time decay (Theta) is table mein zero rakha gaya hai (instant move assume kiya gaya hai); agar din bhi guzarna hai to DTE khud adjust karein.

Expected Premium Move (EPM) Quick estimate vs Full reprice

"Agar index itna move kare, to premium kitna badlega" — ek quick Delta-based andaza deta hai (jo dimaag mein turant calculate kiya ja sakta hai), aur usse poore Black-Scholes reprice se compare karta hai taaki pata chale Delta-only estimate kitna accurate hai. Focus Strike (upar Greeks Panel mein selected) ke liye calculate hota hai.
⚠ Ye field Calculator tab ke "Move index by" se ALAG hai (jaan-boojh kar, taaki dono jagah alag scenario test kar sako). Agar dono same rakhne hain, upar wale buttons se sync kar lo.

Overnight Hold Estimate Delta+Gamma+Theta

Agar aaj position leke kal tak (bina kuch kiye) hold karo, to sirf spot move nahi — Theta (time decay) bhi apna asar dikhayega, chahe spot bilkul na hile. Ye dono ko combine kar ke "net expected move" batata hai.

Error vs Move Size Delta-only vs Delta+Gamma

Chhote moves ke liye Delta-only kaafi accurate hota hai, lekin jaise-jaise move bada hota hai, error badhta jata hai (convexity/Gamma effect) — ye chart wahi dikhata hai.
